HOW TO USE

Three simple steps

1

Choose your device

Select Firestick, Android, Smart TV, Windows, Apple TV, iOS or macOS above.

2

Follow the installation guide

The matching guide opens with steps to install the app on your device.

3

Enter your ORBIT details

After installation, return to the ORBIT Setup Guide and use the username, password and server URL you received.

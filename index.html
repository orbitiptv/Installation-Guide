Installation instructions

Follow the guide for your device. Contact ORBIT TV support when you need help with login details or setup.

📺 Android TV / Fire TV Stick

Install the Downloader app from your device store.

Open Settings and allow installation from unknown sources for Downloader.

Open Downloader, enter one of the 7-digit codes above and press Go.

Download the APK, select Install, then open the player and enter your ORBIT TV username and password.

📱 Android phone / tablet

Scan the QR code next to your preferred player or tap Direct Download.

When Android asks for permission, allow your browser to install unknown apps.

Open the downloaded APK, install it and log in with the details supplied by ORBIT TV.

🍎 iPhone / iPad — install as app

Open the ORBIT iPhone Player link in Safari.

Tap Share — the square icon with the upward arrow.

Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

OrbitTV will appear on your Home Screen and open like an app.

💻 Windows / Mac / browser

Open one of the ORBIT web players in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera or Safari.

Enter your username and password. The server address is hidden in supported ORBIT players.

To install this page as an app, use the Install button or your browser menu.

Security: Download only from the links displayed on this official ORBIT TV page. Android may show an “unknown source” warning because APK files are installed outside Google Play.

Frequently asked questions

Which player should I choose?

Start with ORBIT BUM PRO V2 or ORBIT PLAYER V10 on Android TV and Firestick. Use the ORBIT iPhone Player on iOS, or a web player on computers.

The player does not connect. What should I check?

Check the internet connection, username and password. Restart the player. If your internet provider blocks the connection, try a trusted VPN and contact support.

Can I install the same subscription on multiple devices?

The standard ORBIT TV package includes up to three simultaneous connections. Use the login details supplied with your subscription.

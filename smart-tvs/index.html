How to install an IPTV player on Samsung and LG Smart TVs

A simple step-by-step guide

This ORBIT guide shows how to install a compatible IPTV player directly on a Samsung or LG television, enter your ORBIT details and start watching without an additional device.

ORBIT IPTVSmart TV Setup Guide

Why use IPTV directly on your Smart TV?

If your television supports a compatible app, installing it directly on the TV is a simple way to use the service without an additional box or stick.

No additional device
Remote control navigation
EPG support
HD / 4K playback
Easy to use

Samsung Smart TV

On newer Samsung TVs, you can usually find the app in the Apps menu.

1
Open Apps

Open Samsung Apps from the television home screen and start a search.

2
Find an IPTV player

Search for IPTV Smarters Pro or another player you use with your ORBIT account.

3
Install and launch

Select Install, wait for completion and then open the app.

LG Smart TV

On LG televisions, apps are downloaded through the LG Content Store.

1
Open Content Store

Launch LG Content Store from the television main menu.

2
Search for apps

Find a compatible IPTV player available for your webOS model.

3
Install and open

Complete installation and launch the app to enter your details.

Troubleshooting

The app will not install

  • Check that the TV software is up to date.
  • Check the available storage for apps.
  • If the app is unavailable, availability may depend on your region or TV model.

The app will not launch

  • Restart the television.
  • Clear the app cache if your TV supports this.
  • Uninstall the app and reinstall it.

Login error

  • Check your username and password.
  • Check the server URL.
  • Enter the details exactly, without extra spaces.

Buffering or freezing

  • Use Ethernet if possible.
  • Restart your router and TV.
  • Check your internet connection speed and stability.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find an IPTV app on my television?

Use Apps search on Samsung TVs and LG Content Store on LG TVs.

Why is the app unavailable on my TV?

App availability can depend on your country, television model and operating system version.

Do I need a computer?

No, if the app is available in the television official app store. A computer is needed only for special installation methods.

How long does installation take?

Installation normally takes only a few minutes.

What if it still does not work?

Contact ORBIT support with your television model and the name of the app you use.
